Walking in Love
"And now these three remain: faith, hope and love. But the greatest of these is love."
1 Corinthians 13:13
As we begin this new year, we focus on the greatest fruit of the Spirit—Love. God's love transforms us from the inside out, empowering us to love others as He has loved us. Let's pause and pray together:

Heavenly Father, thank You for Your unfailing love that never gives up on us. As we step into this new year, fill our hearts with Your love so that it overflows to everyone around us. Help us to be patient, kind, and selfless—reflecting Your love in all we do.
In Jesus' Name, Amen.

The answer is: 15 Characteristics
In 1 Corinthians 13:4-7, Paul describes 15 characteristics of love: patient, kind, does not envy, does not boast, is not proud, does not dishonor others, is not self-seeking, is not easily angered, keeps no record of wrongs, does not delight in evil, rejoices with the truth, always protects, always trusts, always hopes, and always perseveres.
