"Peace I leave with you; my peace I give you. I do not give to you as the world gives."

This Lenten season, take the 31-Day Peace Challenge! Each day in March, we're focusing on Peace—the third fruit of the Spirit—as we prepare our hearts for Easter. Listen to the Daily Prayer in the PRAY app and let God's peace transform your Lenten journey. Let's pray:
