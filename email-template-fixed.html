For years, houses of faith have been under attack, from vandalism and threats to heartbreaking acts of violence. In just the past few months, churches in Michigan and Minnesota have faced unimaginable loss when violence struck during worship.
 

These tragedies remind us that even sacred spaces need protectors, and that preparation is part of good stewardship.

At Right To Bear, we believe that protecting the flock is an act of love. Whether your church already has a safety ministry or is just beginning the journey, we want to help you take the next faithful step.

To make it easier, we've created a free Church Security Risk Checklist, a simple, practical guide to help you identify gaps, strengthen your plan, and begin building a safety ministry rooted in faith and wisdom.

Together, we can help your congregation worship with peace of mind, knowing that those who stand watch never stand alone.
